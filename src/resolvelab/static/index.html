HOW TO READ THESE NUMBERS

“Pass” means either verified recovery or appropriate escalation for a read-only task. These are synthetic, public development cases. A perfect runbook score validates the sandbox; it does not demonstrate model generalization. No training improvement or production adoption is implied.

WHAT THIS EXPERIMENT MEASURES

Generalization to new combinations of known faults in one synthetic sync service. Task templates and record seeds are disjoint across training, development and test. This is one model and one training seed. It does not establish production reliability or generalization to new tools. The test set is now public; future tuning needs a new holdout.

Reproduce a comparison

resolvelab eval \
  --policies reference ollama \
  --seeds 42 73 101 \
  --output artifacts/comparison.json

Reports record model digest, code fingerprint, per-case outcomes, token counts and runtime.

What is real in v0.2?

Local model inference, database mutations, operation receipts, exported traces, LoRA training and measured base-versus-SFT results.

The service and fault scenarios are synthetic. Rate-limit waits advance a virtual clock.

What comes next?

External service adapters, larger held-out task families and repeated training seeds to check whether improvements persist.

The Post-training view documents the first experiment, its failures and the limits of the evidence.
